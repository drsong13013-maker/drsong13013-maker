# Hi, I'm Bingsong Huang 👋

**Postdoctoral Researcher at UCSF** — functional genomics, target discovery & therapeutic vulnerabilities in KRAS-driven cancer.

## About me

I'm an MD/PhD cancer biologist working on CRISPR-based functional genomic screens to find synthetic-lethal vulnerabilities in KRAS-mutant lung cancer. My research background centers on KRAS biology and post-translational modifications (PTMs) — how modifications such as lactylation and succinylation rewire cancer signaling and DNA-damage repair — and on peptide design, which I worked on during my PhD.

More recently, I've begun exploring how AI can help solve unsolved problems in biology — de novo protein design and AI models for target discovery — building on my peptide-design background.
